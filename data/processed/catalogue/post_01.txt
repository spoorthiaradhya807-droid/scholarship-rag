SCHEME: Fee Reimbursement
DEPARTMENT: Social Welfare Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Scheduled Caste [SC]
COURSE: All Courses
INCOME LIMIT: Less Than or equal to 2.5 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Student Bonafied data should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP
DOCUMENTS: Caste and income certificate issued by Revenue Department with RD prefix [15 Digits No]; Aadhaar of Student; SATS ID for PUC students; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details; Student Bonafied data should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP
