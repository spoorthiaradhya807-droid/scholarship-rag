SCHEME: Fee Reimbursement
DEPARTMENT: Tribal Welfare Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Scheduled Tribe [ST]
COURSE: All Courses (Private Seat & Management seat type applications are not eligible)
INCOME LIMIT: Less Than or equal to 2.5 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Student Bonafidedata should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details
DOCUMENTS: Caste and income certificate issued by Revenue Department with RD prefix [15 Digits No]; Aadhaar of Student; NSP OTR (One Time Registration) Number is Manadatory; SATS ID for PUC students
