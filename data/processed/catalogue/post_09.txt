SCHEME: Fee Reimbursement
DEPARTMENT: Karnataka State Brahmin Development Board
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Brahmin, students admitted through counselling (Comed-k & Management seats are not eligible)
COURSE: All Courses (2/3rd of Counselling Fee provided only for counselling courses)
INCOME LIMIT: Below 8 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Entering of EWS certificate details is mandatory; Students who have received scholarship from another department will also be eligible for this scheme.
DOCUMENTS: EWS certificate issued by RD (Revenue Department) [15 Digits No]; Aadhaar of Student; SATS ID for PUC students; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details; Student bonafide data should be received from university/educational board (UUCMS/UNIDATA) to SSP

SCHEME: Maintenance
DEPARTMENT: Karnataka State Brahmin Development Board
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Brahmin, same as above

INCOME LIMIT: Below 8 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Entering of EWS certificate details is mandatory; Students who have received scholarship from another department will also be eligible for this scheme.
DOCUMENTS: Same as above
