SCHEME: Fee Reimbursement
DEPARTMENT: Department of Medical Education
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: ST (Scheduled Tribe)
COURSE: Medical courses (MBBS, BDS, MDS, MD & MS)
INCOME LIMIT: Above 2.5 LPA Below 10 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Students who have received scholarship from another department will not be eligible for this scheme. Validated PUC results are mandatory for this scheme. Student Bonafide data should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP. [KEA- CETಮೂಲಕ ಪ್ರವೇಶ ಪಡೆದವರಿಗೆ ಮಾತ್ರ]
DOCUMENTS: Caste and income certificate issued by Revenue Department with RD prefix [15 Digits No], Aadhaar of Student, Students whose Parent/s is/are working in State/ Central Government or in any Government Undertakings or is/are pensioner/s should produce their parent/s latest Salary Slip/ Certificate., Student Bonafidedata should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP
