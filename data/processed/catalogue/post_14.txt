SCHEME: Chief Minister Nekara Vidyanidhi
DEPARTMENT: Handloom and Textiles Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Child of Handloom / Power loom weaver who is enrolled in Handloom and Textiles Department Database (through Seva Sindhu)
COURSE: All Courses
INCOME LIMIT: No caste & income restriction
KEY CONDITIONS: Student needs to enroll in KUTUMBA portal mandatorily. First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details. Student Bonafidedata should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP. This scheme is not a top-up scheme; the student can only receive the scholarship under any one of the schemes.
DOCUMENTS: Enrollment in KUTUMBA portal, Counselling Details (for First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students admitted through counselling), Student Bonafidedata from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA)
