SCHEME: Fee Concession
DEPARTMENT: Backward Classes Welfare Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Caste: Category-1, Other OBC [2A, 3A, 3B etc]. Girls students studying in Government colleges are not eligible.
COURSE: All Courses (Private Seat & Management seat type applications are not eligible) students who are not eligible for postmatric (PM yasasvi).
INCOME LIMIT: Category-1 – Less Than or equal to 2.5 LPA; Other OBC- Less Than or equal 1 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Percentage: Category-1: min 50%, Other OBC's Categories: min 60%; (Private Seat & Management seat type applications are not eligible)
DOCUMENTS: Caste and income certificate issued by Revenue Department with RD prefix [15 Digits No]; Aadhaar of Student; SATS ID for PUC students; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details; Student Bonafidedata should be received from university/educational board (UUCMS/UNIDATA) to SSP.

SCHEME: Postmatric scholarship for OBC (PM Yasasvi).
DEPARTMENT: Backward Classes Welfare Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: OBC students; Category-1, 2A, 3A, 3B
COURSE: All Courses; Students who have not received scholarship under Fee concession / Postmatric scholarship for NT/SNT
INCOME LIMIT: Category-1 – Less Than or equal to 2.5 LPA; Other OBC- Less Than or equal to 1 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Percentage Category-1: min 60%, Other OBC's Categories: min 70%; In all OBC Scholarship schemes, Students who are studying in Management, private seats in private colleges and COMED-K are not eligible to get the scholarship. The student studying in the Residential college is not eligible.
