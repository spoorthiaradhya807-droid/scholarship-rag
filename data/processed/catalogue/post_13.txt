SCHEME: Chief Minister Raitha Vidyanidhi (CMRVN)
DEPARTMENT: Agriculture Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Children of an agricultural family where someone in that family has agricultural land in one's name; family data must be in KUTUMBA portal and they should have a FRUITS-ID with land; only 2 children per family would be eligible
COURSE: All Courses
INCOME LIMIT: <2.5Lakhs
KEY CONDITIONS: Scholarship should not be paid by any other department/schemes (not a top-up scheme); entry of income certificate details is mandatory; student must be identified as eligible under CMRVN; student must enroll in KUTUMBA portal mandatorily
DOCUMENTS: Student Bonafidedata from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA); Counselling Details (for first & second year diploma lateral entry professional course students admitted through counselling); income certificate

SCHEME: Agriculture Labourer Raitha Vidyanidhi (ALRVN)
DEPARTMENT: Agriculture Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Children of landless agricultural labourers who are engaged in agricultural labour activity and have an MGNREGA card in the name of the student's father or mother; only 2 children per family would be eligible
COURSE: All Courses
INCOME LIMIT: <2.5Lakhs
KEY CONDITIONS: Scholarship should not be paid by any other department/schemes (not a top-up scheme); entry of income certificate details is mandatory; student must be identified as eligible under ALRVN; student must enroll in KUTUMBA portal mandatorily
DOCUMENTS: Student Bonafidedata from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA); Counselling Details (for first & second year diploma lateral entry professional course students admitted through counselling); MGNREGA card; income certificate
