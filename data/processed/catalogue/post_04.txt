SCHEME: Postmatric Scholarship (Fee Reimbursement Scheme) / ಮೆಟ್ರಿಕ್ ನಂತರ ವಿದ್ಯಾರ್ಥಿವೇತನ (ಶುಲ್ಕ ಮರುಪಾವತಿ ಯೋಜನೆ)
DEPARTMENT: Department of Minority Welfare
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Muslims, Christians, Jains, Sikhs, Parsis, Buddhist
COURSE: All Courses (PMS & MCM)
INCOME LIMIT: Less Than or equal to 2.0 LPA for PMS Courses. Less Than or equal to 2.5 LPA for MCM Courses.
KEY CONDITIONS: Students should have scored minimum 50% in previous year and Student should pass in all subjects (should not have Backlogs); The student should be a resident of Karnataka; The student studying in the Residential college is not eligible.
DOCUMENTS: Caste and Income certificate issued by Revenue Department in the name of student. [15 Digits No]; NSP ID is optional; Aadhaar of Student; SATS ID for PUC students; First Year Professional course students & Second Year Diploma lateral entry students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details; Student Bonafide data should be shared from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) with SSP.
