SCHEME: Fee Reimbursement
DEPARTMENT: Department of Technical Education
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Caste ST (Scheduled Tribe); Resident of Karnataka; Not studying in a Residential college
COURSE: BE/B.Tech, B-Arch, ME/MTech, M. Arch & Diploma in Polytechnic (Admission only through KEA-CET & Comed-K)
INCOME LIMIT: Above 2.5 LPA Below 10 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Minimum 50% marks in previous year and passed in all subjects (with no backlogs); Student should be a resident of Karnataka; Not studying in residential college; Admission through KEA-CET & Comed-K (or counselling details for 1st year / lateral entry); Student Bonafide data from University/Educational Board (UUCMS/UNIDATA) to SSP; Parent's salary slip/certificate (if State/Central Govt/undertakings/pensioner) or ITR copy (if business/private employee)
DOCUMENTS: Caste and income certificate issued by Revenue Department with RD prefix [15 Digits No]; Aadhaar of Student; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details; Student Bonafide data should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP; Parent's latest Salary Slip/Certificate or income tax return copy

SCHEME: Fee Reimbursement to Kin of Defence Scholarship (ರಕ್ಷಾದಳ)
DEPARTMENT: Department of Technical Education
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Kin of Defence (Parents working in Indian Defense Wing - Army, Air force, Navy as Non-Commissioned Officer / Junior Commission Officer or other below ranks); No caste & income restriction
COURSE: BE/B.Tech, B-Arch & Diploma in Polytechnic (Admission only through KEA-CET)
INCOME LIMIT: No caste & income restriction
KEY CONDITIONS: Parents working in Indian Defense Wing (Army, Air force, Navy) as Non-Commissioned Officer / Junior Commission Officer or in other below ranks; For first year professional courses and Diploma Lateral entry 2nd year students can submit using counselling details; Student Bonafide data from University/Educational Board (UUCMS/UNIDATA) to SSP; Service certificate issued by Defense Authority (Working Unit)
DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar of Student; For first year professional courses and Diploma Lateral entry 2nd year students can submit application using counselling details; Student Bonafide data should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP; Service Certificate issued by the Defense Authority (Working Unit)

DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar of Student; Student must be a holder of BPL card; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details; Student Bonafide data should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP
