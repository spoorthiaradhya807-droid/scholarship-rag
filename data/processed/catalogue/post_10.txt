SCHEME: Fee Concession
DEPARTMENT: Karnataka State Arya Vysya Development Board
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Arya Vysya caste
COURSE: All Courses (Comed-k & Management seats are not eligible); First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling
INCOME LIMIT: Below 1 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Entering of EWS certificate / Form-G Caste and income certificate details is mandatory; Student bonafide data should be received from university/educational board (UUCMS/UNIDATA) to SSP; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details
DOCUMENTS: EWS certificate issued by RD (Revenue Department) [15 Digits No]; Aadhaar of Student; Aadhaar Seeding with bank account
