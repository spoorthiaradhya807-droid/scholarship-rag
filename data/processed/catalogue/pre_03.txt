SCHEME: PM ಯಶಸ್ವಿ
PM Yasasvi (Class 9th & 10th)
DEPARTMENT: Backward Classes Welfare Department
LEVEL: Pre-matric (Class 1 to 10)
FOR: Backward classes (Category-1, 2A, 3A, 3B)
COURSE: Class 9 or 10
INCOME LIMIT: <= 2.5 lakhs
KEY CONDITIONS: The student must be studying in a government school; The student should be studying in class 9 or 10; The student should have secured A+/ A/ B+/B grades in the previous academic year
DOCUMENTS: SATS ID of student; Aadhaar of Student; Aadhar of Parents; Caste and income certificate RD number issued by Revenue Department in the name of Student

SCHEME: NT / SNT (Class 1st to 10th)
DEPARTMENT: Backward Classes Welfare Department
LEVEL: Pre-matric (Class 1 to 10)
FOR: Students belonging to NT/SNT caste only
COURSE: Class 1st to 10th
INCOME LIMIT: <= 2.5 lakh
KEY CONDITIONS: The student should be studying in class 1st to 10th; Student should be studying in a government, private or aided school
DOCUMENTS: SATS ID of students; Aadhaar of Student; Aadhar of Parents; Caste and income certificate RD number issued by Revenue Department in the name of student
