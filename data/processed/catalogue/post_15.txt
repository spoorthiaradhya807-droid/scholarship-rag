SCHEME: Chief Minister Auto/Cab driver Vidyanidhi Program
DEPARTMENT: Chief Minister Auto/Cab Driver Vidyanidhi Program
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Child of Taxi/Auto driver with Yellow Badge who is enrolled as a driver in Transport Department Database (through Seva Sindhu)
COURSE: All Courses
INCOME LIMIT: No caste & income restriction
KEY CONDITIONS: Student needs to enroll in KUTUMBA portal mandatorily. This scheme is not a top-up scheme. If the student receives a scholarship from any other department, then the Handlooms and Textiles Department's scholarship will not be provided. The student can only receive the scholarship under any one of the schemes.
DOCUMENTS: Student needs to enroll in KUTUMBA portal mandatorily. First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details. Student Bonafidedata should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP.
