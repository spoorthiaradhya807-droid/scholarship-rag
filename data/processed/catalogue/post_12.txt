SCHEME: Educational assistance
DEPARTMENT: Labour Welfare Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Must be a child of those involved in any building construction work. Only 2 children per family would be eligible.
COURSE: All Courses
INCOME LIMIT: No caste & income restriction
KEY CONDITIONS: Only 2 children per family would be eligible; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details; Student Bonafidedata should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP.
DOCUMENTS: Labour / Construction worker ID card should be available in the name of Parent; Aadhaar of Student; SATS ID for PUC students; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course documents.
