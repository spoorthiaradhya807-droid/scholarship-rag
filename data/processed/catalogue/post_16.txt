SCHEME: Chief Minister Meenugaarara Vidyanidhi
DEPARTMENT: Fisheries Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Children of workers involved in any of the fishing occupations
COURSE: All Courses
INCOME LIMIT: No caste & income restriction
KEY CONDITIONS: Student needs to enroll in KUTUMBA portal mandatorily; FRUIT'S ID without land but marked as Fisherman; Student should be the child of a worker involved in any of the fishing occupations; Not a top-up scheme: if the student receives a scholarship from any other department, then the Fisheries Department's scholarship will not be provided (student can only receive the scholarship under any one of the schemes)
DOCUMENTS: Enrollment in KUTUMBA portal; FRUIT'S ID without land but marked as Fisherman; Aadhaar of Student; SATS ID for PUC students; First & Second Year Diploma lateral entry Professional course students who are admitted through counselling can submit their application using Counselling Details; Student Bonafide data should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP
