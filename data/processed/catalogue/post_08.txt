SCHEME: Fee Reimbursement
DEPARTMENT: Collegiate Education Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: ST (Scheduled Tribe)
COURSE: MBA & MCA
INCOME LIMIT: Above 2.5 LPA Below 10 LPA
KEY CONDITIONS: Student Bonafidedata should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP; Students whose Parent/s is/are working in State/ Central Government or in any Government Undertakings or is/are pensioner/s should produce their parent/s latest Salary Slip/ Certificate.
DOCUMENTS: Caste and income certificate issued by Revenue Department with RD prefix [15 Digits No]; Aadhaar of Student; Student Bonafidedata should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP; Parent/s latest Salary Slip/ Certificate (if applicable)

SCHEME: Sanchi Honnamma Scholarship (Scheme for Girl Students who have scored higher marks in 2nd PUC and are studying B. A, B. Com & BBA courses in Govt/Aided Degree colleges.)
DEPARTMENT: Collegiate Education Department
LEVEL: Post-matric (Class 11 and above, including PUC)
FOR: Girl Students; SC/ST & Cat -I - 60 %, Others - 75%
COURSE: Arts / Commerce and other general degree courses in government/aided colleges.
INCOME LIMIT: No caste & income restriction
KEY CONDITIONS: Students who have received scholarship from another department will also be eligible for this scheme.; Student Bonafidedata should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP
DOCUMENTS: Aadhaar of Student; Student Bonafidedata should be received from University/Educational Board (UUCMS or UNIDATA) to SSP
