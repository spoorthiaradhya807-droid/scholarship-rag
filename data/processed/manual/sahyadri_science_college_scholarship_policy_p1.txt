POLICY DOCUMENT FOR SCHOLARSHIP AND FREESHIP

The Vision of Sahyadri Science College is to provide quality higher education to the students of various socio-economic sections. To accomplish this vision, Institute follows a policy for the award of scholarship and freeship to economically poor, backward and minority students as per the Karnataka state government policy. Institute also facilitates students to avail scholarship from non-government agencies as per their norms.

Non-Government Scholarship Schemes:
Jindal Trust and Goldmedal for Rank Holder by the Institution

Procedure to Avail Government Scholarship:
1. The Institute has its own scholarship section which includes Main coordinator and coordinators from each department to create awareness among the students to avail the scholarships.
2. Students are informed to download the scholarship application form from https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in/ and https://scholarships.gov.in/ websites.
3. The filled application form along with supporting documents will be attested by the Head of the Department and the Principal.
4. Filled application along with documents will be scanned and uploaded to the website by the students.
5. District nodal officer scrutinizes the filled forms and documents of every student and releases the scholarship amount directly to the students' bank account.
6. If the student avails Government hostel for accommodation and food, the amount will be sent to the concerned student account by the Government.
