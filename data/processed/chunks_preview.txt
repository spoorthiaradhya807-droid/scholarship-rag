exceed ₹8 lakh. This condition does not apply to PM CARES beneficiaries.

FAQs for ISHAN UDAY SCHOLARSHIP

Can all NER students apply for the scholarship?
Reply:
No, the students with North Eastern domicile are eligible to apply. The
income of the parents of the student should be below Rs. 04.50 lakh per annum.

Are students required to have a domicile certificate of NER ?
Reply:
Yes, student should have a valid Domicile Certificate of any State of NER.

Can Student apply in the UG Scholarship when he has enrolled in Integrated
programme.
Reply:
In case of integrated programmes, the scholarship shall be given for UG
component only.

How many students are selected under Ishan Uday scheme.
Reply:
Ten thousand (10000) per annum.

What is the amount of the scholarship?
Reply:
The scholarship is given at the rate of Rs. 8,000/-per month for 10
months in a year.

FAQs for NATIONAL SCHOLARSHIP FOR POSTGRADUATE STUDIES
What is NSPG Scheme?
Reply:
National Scholarship for Post Graduate Studies (NSPGS) is an umbrella
scheme by merging four diﬀerent scholarship schemes for postgraduate studies
i.e. (i) PG Indira Gandhi single girl child scholarship, (ii) PG scholarship for
university rank holders, (iii) PG scholarship for SC/ST students pursuing
professional courses and (iv) Post Graduate Scholarship for GATE/GPAT.

How can I apply online for scholarship?
Reply:
Students can apply online through National Scholarship Portal (NSP).

Does reservation system is followed in this scholarship? If yes what percentage is
reserved for each respective category?
Reply
Yes. The slots are allocated as per Govt. of India reservation policy.
However, 30% slots will be reserved for the women candidates. Preference is
given to single girl child.

What is the definition of Single Girl Child?
Reply:
A 'Single Girl Child' is deﬁned as the only girl child in the family without
any brothers or sisters. If a family has one or more sons and one daughter, the
daughter is not considered a single girl child. However, a girl scholar who is one
of the twin daughters or fraternal daughters is eligible to apply for the scholarship.

Can Student apply in the PG Scholarship when he has enrolled in Integrated
programme?
Reply:
Yes, the student can apply. In case of integrated programmes, the
scholarship shall be given for PG component only.

How may slots are available in PG Studies?
Reply:
Ten thousand (10000) slots per annum.

What is the amount of PG scholarship?
Reply:
The rate of Rs.15,000/- per month for 10 months in a year.

How many slots are reserved for women candidates?
Reply:
30% slots will be reserved for the women candidates. Preference is given
to single girl child.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) – Prime Minister’s Scholarship Scheme (PMSS)

1. What is the Prime Minister’s Scholarship Scheme (PMSS)?
PMSS is a scholarship scheme launched under the National Defence Fund to encourage technical
and professional education for the wards and widows of personnel of CAPFs, Assam Rifles, and
States/UTs Police personnel martyred in Terror/Naxal attacks.

2. Who is eligible to apply under PMSS?
Eligible applicants include:
 Wards/widows of CAPFs & AR personnel who killed in action or died for causes
attributable to Government service.
 Wards of disabled personnel of CAPFs & AR personnel.
 Wards of personnel in receipt of Gallantry Awards.
 Wards of retired and serving CAPFs & AR personnel (PBOR).
 Wards of States/UTs Police personnel martyred in Terror/Naxal attacks.

=== pmss_prime_ministers_scholarship_scheme_faq.pdf | page 1 | native ===
3. What is the minimum educational qualification required?
 New Applicants: Must have secured at least 60% in the Minimum Entry Qualification
(MEQ) such as 10+2, diploma or graduation.
 Renewal Applicants: Must pass each academic year with at least 50% marks.

4. What is the duration and amount of the scholarship?
 Duration: Up to 5 years (based on course duration).
 Amount: ₹3,000/month for girls and ₹2,500/month for boys (paid annually).

5. Is there a quota for scholarships?
Yes. Each year under fresh category:
 2,000 scholarships (1,000 for boys and 1,000 for girls) for CAPFs & AR categories.
 500 scholarships (250 for boy s and 250 for girls) for States/UTs Police category.
Note: Quotas are not interchangeable between genders.

State Scholarship Portal – Prematric
Department FAQ’s (Frequently Asked Questions)

1. What is the State Scholarship Portal (SSP)?
The State Scholarship Portal (SSP) is an integrated e -Governance platform developed
to automate and streamline the management of various scholarship schemes implemented
by various departments of the Government of Karnataka.

2. What is the SSP Prematric Scholarship?
SSP Prematric Scholarship is a Government of Karnataka initiative that provides
financial assistance to students studying in Classes 1-10th

3. Who are the Sponsoring departments in SSP?
Departments such as Social Welfare, Tribal Welfare, Backward Classe s Welfare,
Department of minorities etc, are some of the Government agencies that are responsible for
implementing various programs and initiatives for the welfare of the socially
underprivileged groups in the state.

=== ssp_prematric_department_faq.pdf | page 1 | native ===
4. What is the role of a Sponsoring department in SSP?
The department’s activities include financial aid schemes and other social development
initiatives. The department operates under the jurisdiction of the Karnataka State
Government.
5. What are the criteria for a Sponsoring department in identifying student
applications?
While applying for a scholarship, a student has to provide Caste & Income details in
the SSP application form. Based on student caste, category, and income criteria set by the
sponsoring departments, applications will be displayed to respective department user logins
under three buckets, i.e., Green, Orange & Red.

updated in SATS portal and are correct.

List of activities to be performed by the University/College Authorities
1. Bonafide data of all students studying in the university need to be pushed to
UNIDATA with correct College, Course, Subject Combination/Discipline,
Course year and Type of Seat details of students . Hence, compare with the
applied data given in CIS portal before pushing the Bonafide data.
2. University prescribed fee structure need to be entered in CIS portal and it
should be ensured that fee structure entered in College Information System
(CIS) are correct and complete. For this, maker and checker need to be
appointed in the university.
3. Previous year result data of all students studying in your university need to be
pushed to UNIDATA with maximum marks, marks obtained, pass percentage
& pass with/without backlog.
4. All students staying in University/College run hostels need to be instructed to
submit their hostel details in SSP – HMIS portal in order to avail Hosteller
